About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Iowa State University and an NSF D4 NRT Fellow, working at the intersection of machine learning, genomics, and biomedical data. My research develops biologically informed and interpretable deep-learning methods for genomic analysis, cancer prognosis, and spatial gene-expression prediction. My work has appeared in venues including IEEE BIBM, ACM-BCB, and Health Information Science and Systems.

Featured Work

bioMoR

A biology-guided Mixture-of-Recursions framework that adaptively allocates computation to informative gene and pathway tokens, improving genomic prediction with fewer parameters and FLOPs.

PATH

A pathway-aware graph transformer that learns patient-specific gene representations from mutation and copy-number data for accurate, interpretable cancer progression prediction.

ContextST

A coordinate-frame-invariant multi-context attention model that combines local tissue, slide-wide, and global context to predict spatial gene expression from histology.
